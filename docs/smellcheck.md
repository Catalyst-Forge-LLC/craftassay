# Smell Check overlay

House overlay on `smellcheck`. Core bans live in the sibling package
or https://smellcheck.dev/rules/core.md

Do not restate the hard bans. Do not fork `core.md`.

## Pronouns

- Landing, marketing, docs, README: **you** for the reader. The
  product speaks. No corporate we. No builder I.
- Posts: same, unless the post is signed.

## Terms usually allowed here (not exhaustive)

| Term | Why it earns its keep |
| --- | --- |
| review / findings / scores / evidence | The job and the labels |
| NR | Missing-evidence score |
| subject | The thing under review, in the skill |
| Cold-eye | Sibling readiness pass |
| report.md | The deliverable |
| friction | The user cost the review names, not hype |
| assay | The job metaphor: a test of composition and quality |

## Protected lines

- What works, what creates friction, and what to improve first.
- Earn the word.
- Spray the prose, not the author.
- Have you smell-checked that?

## Genre extras (house law only)

npm, domain, and GitHub belong on Install, not in the hero or About.
Do not inventory the family as "Sibling of X and Y." Name a sibling
when you are saying what this skill is not.

The name is CraftAssay. You may say what an assay is in ordinary
language: a test of what something is made of, and how good it is.
Do not split the name into two bold words. Do not tell the reader
they can skip a word. Do not lecture them on how to capitalize it.
Budget: two assay mentions on a landing page.
