### F-001 · home.md, introduction.md — exclusive_particle / scope

> A clean Cold-eye pass is not a high CraftAssay score.

Default silent reading: a clean Cold-eye review means the CraftAssay
score is low.

| Stress | Reading |
|--------|---------|
| not a high CraftAssay score | clean Cold-eye implies a poor CraftAssay score |
| not [the same as] a high CraftAssay score | the two reviews answer different questions |

**Rewrites**

- Different jobs: *A clean Cold-eye review does not mean CraftAssay will score the work highly.*
- Inverse kept nearby: *A high CraftAssay score is not release approval.*

Applied the first rewrite on home and introduction.

### F-002 · home.md, about.md, introduction.md, README.md — pronoun_stress

> The subject does not change.

Default silent reading: the topic under discussion stays the same,
or the person being reviewed does not change their mind.

| Stress | Reading |
|--------|---------|
| subject | the reviewed work, or the topic of the sentence |
| does not change | nobody edits it, or the facts stay true |

**Rewrites**

- Source: *The skill does not edit the source.*
- Work: *The reviewed work stays as it is.*

Applied the first rewrite on those surfaces. Install now says *work*
where it used to say *subject* for the thing you point at.
