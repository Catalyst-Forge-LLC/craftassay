# Value as presented

Use this check for discovery surfaces: a public landing page, catalog, README, or equivalent entry point. For a procedure or internal artifact without a discovery surface, record not applicable and explain why. Do not force marketing copy onto an obvious utility or specialist workflow.

Before opening internal specifications, reconstruct the answers available to the intended reader. Preserve that first-reader account, then compare it with examples, setup instructions, and implementation evidence where relevant. An author's explanation can identify intent but cannot fill a gap in the public page.

| Question | Look for |
| --- | --- |
| Why me? | Recognizable person, task, or situation, with realistic expertise and setup requirements |
| What is it? | Product form and job, such as an agent skill, command-line tool, app, or specification |
| So what? | The inconvenience, uncertainty, repeated effort, or missed opportunity that makes the job matter |
| So that? | The decision, action, retained knowledge, or other bounded outcome the result enables |
| Show me | Representative input, output, and consequence, with illustrations distinguished from observed results |
| Why this approach? | A supported reason to choose this workflow, including relevant tradeoffs, without invented competitive superiority |
| What next? | A first action, its prerequisites, and what successful first use produces |

In coverage.md, record each answer as **explicit**, **inferable**, **missing**, **contradictory**, or **not applicable**, with its source and reader consequence. These statuses are observations, not scores. Inferable can be sufficient: a visible stopwatch with Start and Reset need not explain why timing a task is useful. Not applicable needs a reason, not a guessed answer.

In report.md, write a short **Value as presented** paragraph explaining whether the reader can recognize their situation, understand the bounded result, and choose a next step. State what the product appears to offer separately from how well the page communicates it. Weak explanation does not establish that the product has little value or that market demand is absent.

Map the evidence to existing dimensions. Relevance and adoption burden inform Utility. Comprehension informs Clarity. Obscured examples and visual hierarchy inform Aesthetics when the interface was inspected. Unsupported promises inform Accuracy. Record one finding per underlying problem and reference that finding from affected dimensions. Do not multiply priority or severity by the number of dimensions it touches. Use NR when evidence is insufficient.

## Calibration

- A specialist parser names its input format, output schema, prerequisites, and example. A general visitor's unfamiliarity is not a defect for its stated specialist audience.
- A timer visibly starts, pauses, and resets. Its purpose can be inferable. Do not demand a slogan, competitor table, or elaborate persona.
- A polished page promises guaranteed results without evidence. Record a claim-risk finding. Polish does not validate the claim.
- A useful runnable tool has a vague opening. Identify the missing public explanation under Clarity, and preserve the evidence that the workflow itself is useful.
- A complete entry page has no material gap. Say so. Do not invent a positioning task to populate the findings register.

This check needs no other review skill, proprietary service, or network access when the supplied evidence is local.
