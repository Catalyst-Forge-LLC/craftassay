`report.md` is what you read. The skill writes it.

1. Main judgment
2. Coverage and limits
3. Scoring definitions
4. Scorecard
5. Per-project critique
6. Highest-confidence errors and claim issues
7. Prioritized actions
8. Comparison with baseline, when one exists

Every material finding has an id, a classification, a surface, evidence, a consequence, a priority, and a smallest sufficient change. Findings without an action are dropped.

Scores are whole numbers from 1 to 10, or NR. Accuracy is not a runtime bug rate. A clean review is allowed.

The full contract is [specification v1.1](https://github.com/Catalyst-Forge-LLC/craftassay/blob/main/craftassay-review-spec-v1.1.md), rubric v1.0.
