description: Why the name combines craft with assay, and why the report leaves the subject alone.
order: 3
---

**Craft** is the care and judgment in what someone builds. **Assay** is a structured examination of its qualities. Readers should not need the second word. The service is a review with findings, scores, evidence, and a way to compare versions.

Use the exact capitalization **CraftAssay**. The report is an evidence-backed assessment, not a certification and not a guarantee of reliability.

The subject does not change. Implementing the findings is a later, separate request.

[Cold-eye](https://coldeye.dev) is the readiness pass. CraftAssay is the broader critique. They can share a finding without being the same job.
