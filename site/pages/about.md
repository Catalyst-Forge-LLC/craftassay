An assay is a test of composition and quality. You take a sample, you measure what is actually there, and you say how it stands.

CraftAssay is that kind of review: findings, scores, evidence, and a way to compare versions.

The report is an assessment, not a certification and not a guarantee of reliability.

The skill does not edit the source. Implementing the findings is a later, separate request.

[Cold-eye](https://coldeye.dev) is the readiness pass. CraftAssay is the broader critique. They can share a finding without being the same job.
