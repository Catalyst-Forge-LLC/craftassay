# HarborNote

Notes that stay with you.

HarborNote is the notes app for people who are tired of clouds.
Install it, type, and you are done. Notes stay on this device
unless you turn on sync.

[Start free](https://harbornote.example/signup)

## Why teams switch

- End-to-end encryption
- Works offline
- Optional sync to devices you choose
- Used by thousands of teams
