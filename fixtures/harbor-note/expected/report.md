## HarborNote (`harbornote.example`)

**User and job:** A person who wants local notes without a cloud.
**What works and should remain:** The offline line is a clear job.
**Review:** The hero says nothing leaves the machine, then the same
page sells automatic multi-device sync and a signup URL.
**Material findings:** harbornote-F001, harbornote-F002, harbornote-F003.
**Recommended changes, in order:** Bound the privacy claim. Repair or
drop the privacy whitepaper link. Put the job before the logo-scale
promise.
**Boundary to preserve:** Local-first notes remain the product.
**Evidence and limits:** Fixture text only.

## Highest-confidence errors and claim issues

The privacy absolute and the 404 whitepaper link are observed on
this page. Uniqueness is NR because no comparison was performed.

## Prioritized actions

1. Replace "Nothing leaves your machine. Ever." with what actually
   stays local.
2. Fix or remove the privacy whitepaper link.
3. Lead with the notes job before the universal claim.
